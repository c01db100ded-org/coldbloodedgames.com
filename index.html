How to play — Crown & Outpost

  1. Follow the 60–90 second guided first-run tutorial to learn the gate.
  2. Scout the wave banner and deploy your six squads — Shieldbearer, Oathkeeper, and the Signal Bell among them.
  3. Train, promote, and spend Valor Marks in the War Tent.
  4. Fight through all seven chapters and their bosses, then push into Endless mode beyond victory.
  5. Check back daily for login rewards, weekly challenges, and offline leaderboards.
