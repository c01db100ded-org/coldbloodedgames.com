Crown & Outpost — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-07 · Version 1.0

Summary

Crown & Outpost is a free, offline, single-player game. We do not collect, store, share, or sell any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no in-app purchases, and no third-party SDKs that collect data. The game works fully offline.

Data we collect

None. Specifically:

  • No account registration, sign-in, or user profiles.
  • No email addresses, names, phone numbers, or other identifiers.
  • No precise or approximate location.
  • No photos, videos, files, contacts, or device contents.
  • No advertising IDs or analytics identifiers.
  • No crash reports or diagnostics transmitted off-device.
  • No in-app purchases, subscriptions, or payment information.

Data stored on your device

To make the game playable, the app saves progress locally on your device only (campaign progress, Valor Marks, doctrine upgrades, settings). This data stays in the app's private storage on your device, is never transmitted, and is deleted when you uninstall the app or use the in-game reset option.

Data sharing

There is nothing to share. The app has no network capability and makes no network requests. No data is shared with third parties, advertisers, or analytics providers.

Children's privacy

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

Permissions

The app requests no sensitive permissions (no location, camera, microphone, contacts, storage, or phone access). It requires no internet permission.

Changes to this policy

If the game ever gains features that collect data (for example accounts or analytics), this policy will be updated and the effective date above will change before such a version is released.

Contact

Developer: Coldblooded Games. Questions about this policy: contact us at support@coldbloodedgames.com or through the support email listed on the Google Play Store listing.
